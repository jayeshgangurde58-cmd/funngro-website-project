GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers.

Do I need experience to get started?＋

Not for every opportunity. Read each campaign brief carefully because requirements and eligibility can differ.

What kind of work can I explore?＋

Depending on current campaigns, opportunities may include content, brand promotion, surveys, referrals, sampling and app testing.

How do rewards work?＋

Any reward, approval process and payout method are defined by the individual campaign. Review the terms before participating.

Is it safe to participate?＋

Use official Funngro channels, protect your personal information, involve a parent or guardian where appropriate, and never pay someone to access a task.
