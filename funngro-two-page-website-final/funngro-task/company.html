LET'S CLEAR THINGS UP

Company FAQs.

What kind of campaigns can we run?＋

Depending on your objectives, options may include promotion, content, surveys, sampling, referrals and app testing.

How do we get started?＋

Contact the Funngro team with your objective, target audience, preferred timeline and indicative budget to discuss a suitable brief.

Can we define campaign requirements?＋

Yes. Campaign requirements, participant eligibility, deliverables, timelines and review criteria should be agreed before activation.

How are results evaluated?＋

Measurement depends on the campaign. Agree on the relevant indicators and reporting format with the team before launch.
